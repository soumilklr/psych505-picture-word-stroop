Picture-Word Stroop Task

On each trial, you will first see a cue telling you to pay attention to either the picture or the word. Then you will briefly see a picture with a word on top of it. After that, you will see a single test item and decide whether it is related or unrelated to the part you were told to attend to.

Example: if the cue says Attend to the picture, and you see a picture of a dog with the word RAIN, then you should remember the dog and ignore the word rain.

If the next item is a bone, that would be related. If the next item is an umbrella, that would be unrelated.

  • Use the on screen buttons or press F for Related and J for Unrelated.
  • You will get a short practice block with feedback first.
  • The main task has 20 trials and should feel much more readable than the earlier version.

Research versions of this task used a 1000 ms cue, a 700 ms picture-word display, a short delay before the test item, and a response window of up to 2000 ms. This version follows that general structure, but uses clearer screens and fewer trials.

Respond only when the single test item appears.

Try to respond as quickly and accurately as you can. Do not guess until the single test item appears.
