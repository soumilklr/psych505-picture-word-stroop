Picture-Word Stroop Task

In this study, you will see a picture with a word placed on top of it. On each trial, a cue will tell you whether to attend to the picture or the word. You should ignore the other part of the display.

After a short delay, you will see a single test item. Your job is to decide whether that test item is related or unrelated to the part you were told to attend to.

  • Use the Related and Unrelated buttons, or press F for Related and J for Unrelated.
  • The task includes a short practice phase with feedback, followed by the main task without feedback.
  • Please do the task in a quiet place on a laptop or desktop browser if possible.

This browser task stores trial-by-trial responses in a Google Sheet when the script URL is connected. A backup CSV can also be downloaded at the end.

Attend to the instructed part only. Respond as quickly and accurately as you can.
